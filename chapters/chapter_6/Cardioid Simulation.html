What is a Cardioid?

A cardioid is a mathematical curve that resembles a heart shape. It can be generated in several ways:

  • As the path traced by a point on the circumference of a circle rolling around another circle of the same radius
  • As the envelope of circles whose centers lie on a given circle and which pass through a fixed point on that circle
  • As the polar equation: r = a(1 + cos θ)

In this simulation, we're generating the cardioid by connecting points on a circle. Each point is connected to another point determined by multiplying its index by the multiplier value.
