1. Linearity

Integrals preserve vector space operations. The integral of a linear combination equals the linear combination of integrals.

2. Domain Additivity

If R₁ and R₂ overlap only on boundaries, the integral over their union equals the sum of integrals.

3. Bounding / Estimation

The integral is bounded between the minimum and maximum values of f scaled by the region's area.

4. Fubini's Theorem

For continuous f on a rectangle, iterated integrals in either order yield the same result.
