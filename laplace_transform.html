Why Poles & Zeros Matter

×
Poles pull surface → ∞

At a pole, the denominator of F(s) vanishes, creating a resonance "spike". Each pole contributes an exponential mode to the time-domain response.

○
Zeros flatten surface → 0

At a zero, the numerator vanishes, canceling response. Zeros attenuate specific frequencies and shape transient behavior.
