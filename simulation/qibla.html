Calculation Formula

Qibla Coordinates: φ₀ = 21°25.2' N, λ₀ = 39°49.2' E

l = λ - λ₀ (Central East)

p = sin(φ) / tan(l)

q = cos(φ) * tan(φ₀) / sin(l)

tan(i) = p - q

Where i is the direction of Qibla from North
