Non-Newtonian Liquid Flow Near a Stagnation Point

Interactive simulation based on the 1958 paper by A.C. Srivastava, visualizing the effect of cross-viscosity on boundary layer thickness and flow characteristics.

Observation: The boundary layer thickness decreases as K increases from 0 to ~0.15, then increases with further increase in K.

Parameter Analysis

K δ (Boundary Layer) φ''(0) (Shear)

The non-dimensional parameter K = μ₀a/μ represents the ratio of cross-viscosity (μ₀) to viscosity (μ), scaled by the flow constant (a).

δ (Boundary Layer Thickness) and φ''(0) (Wall Shear) vs K

As K increases, the shearing stress at the wall decreases, while the normal stress remains independent of both viscosity and cross-viscosity.

Simulation based on: "The Flow of a Non-Newtonian Liquid Near a Stagnation Point" by Abinash Chandra Srivastava (1958)
