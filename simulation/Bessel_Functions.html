Why Bessel?

Separating the Laplace and Helmholtz equations in cylindrical (and spherical) coordinates produces Bessel's differential equation. Its solutions describe vibrating drumheads, heat flow in cylinders, electromagnetic waveguides, diffraction patterns (the Airy disk), FM modulation, and radial point-spread functions in optics.

$$y = A\,J_n(x) + B\,Y_n(x), \qquad x^2y'' + xy' + (x^2-n^2)y = 0$$

  • ber, bei, ker, kei — Kelvin (eddy currents)

Section 01 · Schaum §24.1–24.6

Ordinary Bessel Functions \(J_n(x),\;Y_n(x)\)

Solutions of Bessel's equation of integer order \(n\): the oscillatory radial eigenfunctions of the cylinder.

24.1 · The differential equation
$$x^2 y'' + x y' + (x^2 - n^2) y = 0$$

For integer \(n\) the general solution on \((0,\infty)\) is \(y = A J_n(x) + B Y_n(x)\). \(J_n\) is regular at the origin; \(Y_n\) (Neumann) is singular.

24.2 · Series, first kind
$$J_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k\,(x/2)^{\,n+2k}}{k!\,(n+k)!}$$

Entire in \(x\) (up to \(x^n\)); \(J_n(0)=\delta_{n0}\); infinitely many simple real zeros \(\alpha_{n,m}\).

24.3 · Special integer cases
$$J_0(x)=1-\frac{x^2}{2^2}+\frac{x^4}{2^2\cdot4^2}-\frac{x^6}{2^2\cdot4^2\cdot6^2}+\cdots$$ $$J_1(x)=\frac{x}{2}\left(1-\frac{x^2}{2\cdot4}+\frac{x^4}{2\cdot4\cdot4\cdot6}-\cdots\right)$$

First zeros: \(\alpha_{0,1}=2.40483\), \(\alpha_{1,1}=3.83171\). Derivative: \(J_0'(x)=-J_1(x)\).

24.4 · Second kind (Neumann)
$$Y_n(x)=\frac{2}{\pi}\Big(\ln\frac{x}{2}+\gamma\Big)J_n(x)-\frac{1}{\pi}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{(n-k-1)!}{k!}\Big(\frac{x}{2}\Big)^{2k-n}-\frac{1}{\pi}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^k\,(H_k+H_{n+k})}{k!\,(n+k)!}\Big(\frac{x}{2}\Big)^{n+2k}$$

\(\gamma=0.57721\dots\), \(H_k\) harmonic numbers. Wronskian \(W\{J_n,Y_n\}=\frac{2}{\pi x}\). \(J_{-n}=(-1)^nJ_n\).

Section 02 · Schaum §24.13–24.16

Modified Bessel Functions \(I_n(x),\;K_n(x)\)

Bessel functions of imaginary argument — exponentially growing/decaying radial modes.

24.13 · Modified equation
$$x^2 y'' + x y' - (x^2 + n^2) y = 0$$

Bessel's equation with \(x\to ix\). Solutions: \(I_n\) (finite at 0), \(K_n\) (decaying at \(\infty\)).

24.14 · First kind
$$I_n(x) = i^{-n} J_n(ix) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(x/2)^{n+2k}}{k!\,(n+k)!}$$

All terms positive ⇒ monotone growth \(I_n\sim \frac{e^x}{\sqrt{2\pi x}}\). \(I_{-n}=I_n\).

24.15 · Second kind (Macdonald)
$$K_n(x)=\frac{\pi}{2}\,i^{\,n+1}H_n^{(1)}(ix)=\lim_{\nu\to n}\frac{\pi}{2}\,\frac{I_{-\nu}(x)-I_\nu(x)}{\sin \nu\pi}$$

\(K_0\sim -\ln(x/2)-\gamma\), \(K_n\sim \tfrac12 (n-1)!(2/x)^n\); decay \(K_n\sim\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2x}}e^{-x}\). \(W\{I_n,K_n\}=-\frac1x\).

Duality at a glance
$$J_n(x)\;\xrightarrow{x\,\to\,ix}\; i^{\,n} I_n(x), \qquad \tfrac{\pi}{2}i^{\,n+1}H_n^{(1)}(ix) = K_n(x)$$

Oscillation ↔ exponential behaviour; \((J_n,Y_n)\mapsto(I_n,K_n)\).

Section 03 · Schaum §24.10–24.12

Half-Integer Order & Hankel Functions

Half-integer orders collapse to elementary trigonometry; Hankel functions package \(J_n\pm iY_n\) into travelling cylindrical waves.

24.10 · Half-integer orders
$$J_{1/2}(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\,\sin x, \qquad J_{-1/2}(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\,\cos x$$ $$J_{3/2}(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\left(\frac{\sin x}{x}-\cos x\right)$$

Higher half-integers follow from §05 recurrences; these build the spherical Bessel functions \(j_\ell=\sqrt{\pi/2x}\,J_{\ell+1/2}\).

24.11 · Hankel functions
$$H_n^{(1)}(x)=J_n(x)+i\,Y_n(x), \qquad H_n^{(2)}(x)=J_n(x)-i\,Y_n(x)$$ $$H_n^{(1)}(x)\sim\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\,e^{\,i(x-n\pi/2-\pi/4)}$$

\(H^{(1)}\) = outgoing, \(H^{(2)}\) = incoming cylindrical waves; \(H_n^{(2)}=\overline{H_n^{(1)}}\) for \(x>0\).

Section 04 · Schaum §24.17–24.19

Kelvin Functions \(\operatorname{ber},\operatorname{bei},\operatorname{ker},\operatorname{kei}\)

Real/imaginary parts of Bessel functions on rotated complex rays — AC eddy currents in cylindrical conductors (order 0 shown).

24.17 · Definitions
$$J_n\!\left(x\,e^{3\pi i/4}\right) = \operatorname{ber}_n x + i\,\operatorname{bei}_n x$$ $$e^{-n\pi i/2}K_n\!\left(x\,e^{\pi i/4}\right) = \operatorname{ker}_n x + i\,\operatorname{kei}_n x$$

Rotating the argument by \(3\pi/4\) turns oscillation into growing chirped spirals.

24.18 · Series (order 0)
$$\operatorname{ber} x = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^j (x/2)^{4j}}{[(2j)!]^2}, \qquad \operatorname{bei} x = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^j (x/2)^{4j+2}}{[(2j+1)!]^2}$$

\(\operatorname{ber}0=1,\ \operatorname{bei}0=0,\ \operatorname{kei}0=-\pi/4,\ \operatorname{ker}x\sim-\ln(x/2)-\gamma\).

Section 05 · Schaum §24.7–24.9

Recurrence Formulas & Differential Relations

Ladder identities generating every order from \(J_0,J_1\) — verified numerically below.

For \(J_n\)
$$J_{n-1}+J_{n+1}=\frac{2n}{x}J_n$$ $$J_{n-1}-J_{n+1}=2J_n'$$ $$J_n'=J_{n-1}-\frac{n}{x}J_n=\frac{n}{x}J_n-J_{n+1}$$ $$\big[x^n J_n\big]'=x^n J_{n-1},\qquad \big[x^{-n}J_n\big]'=-x^{-n}J_{n+1}$$

In particular \(J_0'=-J_1\).

For \(I_n\) and \(K_n\)
$$I_{n-1}-I_{n+1}=\frac{2n}{x}I_n,\qquad I_{n-1}+I_{n+1}=2I_n'$$ $$\big[x^n I_n\big]'=x^n I_{n-1},\qquad \big[x^{-n}I_n\big]'=x^{-n}I_{n+1}$$ $$K_n'=-K_{n-1}-\frac{n}{x}K_n=-\tfrac12(K_{n-1}+K_{n+1})$$

Sign flips vs. \(J_n\) — consequence of \(x\to ix\).

Section 06 · Schaum §24.20–24.23

Generating Functions & Integral Representations

One generating function encodes every \(J_n\); Jacobi–Anger decomposes plane waves into cylindrical waves.

24.20 · Generating function
$$e^{\frac{x}{2}\left(t-\frac{1}{t}\right)} = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} J_n(x)\,t^n$$

\(t=1\) gives \(\sum_{-\infty}^{\infty}J_n(x)=1\) — verified live below.

24.21 · Jacobi–Anger
$$e^{ix\cos\theta}=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} i^{\,n}J_n(x)e^{in\theta}$$ $$\cos(x\sin\theta)=J_0(x)+2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}J_{2k}(x)\cos 2k\theta$$
24.22 · Bessel's integral
$$J_n(x)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{\pi}\cos(n\theta - x\sin\theta)\,d\theta$$

Poisson: \(J_n(x)=\frac{(x/2)^n}{\sqrt{\pi}\,\Gamma(n+\tfrac12)}\int_0^{\pi}\cos(x\cos\theta)\sin^{2n}\theta\,d\theta\).

24.23 · Laplace-type integrals
$$\int_0^{\infty} e^{-at}J_0(bt)\,dt=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}},\qquad \int_0^{\infty} J_0(x)\,dx = 1$$
\(\sum_{n=-N}^{N}J_n(x)\to 1\) (case \(t=1\))
\(N=\)4

Odd orders cancel (\(J_{-n}=(-1)^nJ_n\)); even orders accumulate to 1.

Section 07 · Schaum §24.24–24.28

Asymptotic Expansions, Orthogonality & Fourier–Bessel Series

Large/small-argument limits, orthogonality of radial eigenfunctions, and the resulting generalized Fourier series.

24.24 · Large \(x\)
$$J_n\sim\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\cos\!\left(x-\frac{n\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right),\quad Y_n\sim\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\sin\!\left(x-\frac{n\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$$ $$I_n\sim\frac{e^x}{\sqrt{2\pi x}},\qquad K_n\sim\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2x}}\,e^{-x}$$
24.25 · Small \(x\)
$$J_n\sim\frac{1}{n!}\Big(\frac{x}{2}\Big)^n,\quad Y_0\sim\frac{2}{\pi}\ln\frac{x}{2},\quad Y_n\sim-\frac{(n-1)!}{\pi}\Big(\frac{2}{x}\Big)^n$$ $$K_0\sim-\ln\frac{x}{2}-\gamma,\qquad K_n\sim\frac{(n-1)!}{2}\Big(\frac{2}{x}\Big)^n$$
24.26 · Orthogonality
$$\int_0^1 r\,J_n(\alpha_{n,m}r)J_n(\alpha_{n,k}r)\,dr = \begin{cases} 0, & m\ne k\\[4pt] \tfrac12\big[J_{n+1}(\alpha_{n,m})\big]^2, & m=k \end{cases}$$
24.27 · Fourier–Bessel series
$$f(r)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty} c_m J_n(\alpha_{n,m}r),\quad c_m=\frac{2}{\big[J_{n+1}(\alpha_{n,m})\big]^2}\int_0^1 r\,f(r)J_n(\alpha_{n,m}r)\,dr$$
