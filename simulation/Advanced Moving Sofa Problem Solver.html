Optimization Algorithm

This implementation uses a hybrid approach combining Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) with Fourier descriptor parameterization to find optimal sofa shapes.

1 Particle Initialization

Initialize a swarm of particles with random Fourier coefficients representing potential sofa shapes.

2 Fitness Evaluation

Evaluate each particle based on area and feasibility constraints at multiple rotation angles.

3 Velocity Update

Update particle velocities based on personal best and global best positions with inertia.

4 Position Update

Move particles to new positions and apply mutation for diversity.

Advanced Features

  • Adaptive inertia weight for better convergence
  • Constraint handling with penalty functions
  • Mutation operator to escape local optima
  • Multi-start optimization with different initial conditions
