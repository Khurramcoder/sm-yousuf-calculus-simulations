Cube Decomposition

When we expand (a+b)³, we get four distinct components:

a³

The cube with side length a

Volume: a × a × a = a³

3a²b

Three rectangular prisms with dimensions a × a × b

Volume: 3 × (a × a × b) = 3a²b

3ab²

Three rectangular prisms with dimensions a × b × b

Volume: 3 × (a × b × b) = 3ab²

b³

The cube with side length b

Volume: b × b × b = b³
