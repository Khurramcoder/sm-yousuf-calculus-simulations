Centre of Curvature, Osculating Circle & Evolute Explorer

Theorem

For a curve \(y=f(x)\), \[ h=x-\frac{y'(1+y'^2)}{y''}, \qquad k=y+\frac{1+y'^2}{y''} \] give the coordinates of the centre of curvature.

Interactive Example

Curve: \(y=x^2/8\)

Proof Outline

  1. Find tangent slope \(y'\).
  2. Construct the normal line.
  3. Compute curvature: \[ \kappa=\frac{|y''|}{(1+y'^2)^{3/2}} \]
  4. Radius: \[ \rho=\frac1\kappa \]
  5. Move distance \(\rho\) along the normal.
  6. The locus of centres is the evolute.
