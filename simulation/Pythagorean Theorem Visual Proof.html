How It Works

1 The Pythagorean theorem states that in a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse (c) equals the sum of squares of the other two sides (a and b).
2 This visualization shows how the areas of squares built on sides a and b can be rearranged to exactly fill the square built on the hypotenuse c.
3 Use the sliders to change the lengths of sides a and b, and watch how the relationship a² + b² = c² always holds true.
4 The "Switch to Area View" button shows the actual area comparison, proving that the combined area of the two smaller squares equals the area of the largest square.
