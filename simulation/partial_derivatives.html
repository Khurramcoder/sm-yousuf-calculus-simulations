Core Concept

When finding a partial derivative, you look at how a multi-variable function changes along one axis only, treating all other variables as fixed constants.

  • w.r.t $x$: We treat y as a frozen number. The red plane cuts through the surface parallel to the x-axis, producing a 2D curve. The slope of the line tangent to this curve is $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$.
  • w.r.t $y$: We treat x as a frozen number. The green plane cuts through parallel to the y-axis. The slope of the line tangent to this curve is $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$.
